THE STORY BEHIND THE FLAME

More Than Just a Candle

Anusmaran is a lifestyle venture inspired by the connection between fragrance, memories and mindful living. Our product concepts bring together aromatic experiences and clay containers with a focus on thoughtful design and environmental responsibility.

We aim to make everyday moments more meaningful while exploring opportunities for positive environmental and social impact.

Our Fragrance Collection

Four distinctive candle concepts inspired by botanical ingredients and aromatic experiences.

01 NATURE
❋

Prakriti

An earthy herbal fragrance featuring vetiver(Ushira), neem and tulsi, inspired by traditional Indian ways of keeping the home fresh and cool. Prevents insects, purifies the air and helps reduce body heat with its cooling effect.

USHIRA NEEM TULSI
02 CALM
❋

Sukoon

A calming blend of eucalyptus, mint and jatamansi, created to bring a peaceful, soothing atmosphere to your space. Calms the mind, reduce stress and anxiety and supports mental well-being.

EUCALYPTUS MINT JATAMANSI
03 MINDFULNESS
❋

Sankalp

A warm, spicy-fresh blend of black pepper, mint and angelica, inspired by the idea of sankalp (resolve) and made for mindful moments and fresh Starts. Designed to help reduce nicotine addiction.

BLACK PEPPER MINT ANGELICA
04 COMFORT
❋

Rahat

A comforting blend of tulsi, mint and clove with warm herbal-spiced notes that bring a feeling of ease (rahat) to your home. Helps reduce cold and cough.

TULSI MINT CLOVE
